DREESHFILMEDIT is an independent cinematic studio based right here in Shelby, North Carolina. I approach visual storytelling with a fresh lens, taking deliberate time to capture how a finished piece actually feels.

My primary camera is an advanced iPhone paired with the Blackmagic Camera app. This grants absolute control over manual focus, shutter angle, ISO, and ProRes color science—delivering rich, authentic cinema quality without cumbersome lighting rigs or a massive crew getting in your way.
